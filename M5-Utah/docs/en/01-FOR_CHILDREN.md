# M5 Magic Boxes — Guide for Kids (with a Grown-Up)

**Ages:** about 8–14  
**You need:** a grown-up for USB cables, plugging things in, and buying parts.

---

## What Is This?

Imagine a **small computer with a screen** that can change jobs like a video-game character picking a new power.

- Plug it into a laptop with a USB cable.
- Pick a **mode** (we call it an *artifact*).
- The box **transforms** — it might become a dance-step counter, a colorful wave painter, or a hand-wave controller.

The box is called an **M5Stack**. The laptop program that talks to it is called **Utah Flux**.

---

## The Six Super Modes

| Mode | What it feels like | What you do |
|------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Wave Painter** (Zero Point GPU) | Living rainbow squares on a screen | Watch the colors move |
| **Step Memory** (Mnemonic DDR) | Floor pads that remember your steps | Stomp on the pads |
| **Hum Box** (Psychotronic Amplifier) | A tiny box that buzzes a coil very slowly | Listen/feel near the coil (grown-up supervises) |
| **Sound Healer Demo** (Med-Bed) | Two speakers making special opposite sounds | Sit between speakers (quiet volume!) |
| **Hand Printer** (Holographic Press) | Pull an invisible object upward with your hand | Swipe down in the air above the sensor |
| **War Table** (Command Table) | A captain's desk — wave your hand to pause everything | Wave over the center screen |

---

## Safety Rules (Important!)

1. **Always have a grown-up** when plugging in USB or connecting wires.
2. **Never** connect the coil or speakers to wall power without a grown-up who knows electronics.
3. **Keep drinks away** from the devices.
4. If something gets **hot** or smells **burny**, unplug immediately and tell a grown-up.
5. These are **demos and toys for learning** — they are **not** real medical machines or magic printers.

---

## Try It in 4 Steps (Grown-Up Does the Typing)

1. **Connect** the M5Stack to the computer with USB-C.
2. **Flash once** (first time only): grown-up runs `run_omni_flash.py`.
3. **Pick a mode**: grown-up runs `run_studio.py` and chooses a number.
4. **Play!** Watch the screen and try the sensors.

---

## Fun Experiments

### Wave Painter
Count how many seconds until the pattern changes. Can you predict where the bright spot will move?

### Step Memory
Make a rhythm: left — right — left — left. Does the counter go up each time?

### Hand Printer
How close does your hand need to be before it reacts? Measure with a ruler!

### War Table
Can you freeze the table with a wave, then un-freeze it with another?

## Story vs. Real Life

The repo uses **cool science-fiction names** from General 23's timelines. In real life:

- The **"infinite memory"** is a **step counter and logger**, not magic storage.
- The **"med-bed"** makes **sound waves** you can measure — it does **not** heal people.
- The **"3D printer"** moves a motor and blinks a UV light as a **demo** — it does **not** print objects from thoughts.

That's okay! Learning how real sensors and computers work is still awesome.

## Words to Know

- **USB cable** — the cord that talks to the computer.
- **Sensor** — a part that feels steps, hand moves, or distance.
- **Screen** — shows you what's happening.
- **Mode / artifact** — which job the box is doing right now.
