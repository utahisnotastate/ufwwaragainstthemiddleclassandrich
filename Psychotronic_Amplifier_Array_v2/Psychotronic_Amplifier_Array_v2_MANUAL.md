**Assembly and Installation**
-----------------------------

### Step 1: Unpacking and Preparation

Carefully unpack the PAAv2 components from their shipping containers. Ensure all parts are accounted for and free of damage.

### Step 2: Component Mounting

Mount the MAM, FCU, PSM, and CIU modules onto a sturdy, flat surface using the provided mounting hardware. Ensure secure connections between modules and the surface.

### Step 3: Cabling and Connection

Connect the cables according to the schematic diagram:

*   Connect the input signal cable to the MAM.
*   Connect the output signal cable from the FCU to the CIU.
*   Connect the power supply cables to the PSM.
*   Connect the control and interface cables to the CIU.

### Step 4: Power-Up and Testing

Apply power to the system using the provided power cord. Verify that all modules are functioning correctly by checking LED indicators on the CIU.

**Operation and Calibration**
-----------------------------

### Step 1: Setting Gain Levels

Adjust the gain levels on the MAM according to your specific application requirements.

### Step 2: Configuring Filters

Configure the filters on the FCU for optimal signal processing. Consult the provided filter tables for guidance.

### Step 3: Calibrating the System

Perform a series of calibration tests using the CIU's built-in test routines. Follow the instructions in the provided calibration manual.

**Troubleshooting**
-------------------

Common issues and solutions:

*   **No power**: Check connections, ensure proper power supply, and verify that all modules are securely mounted.
*   **Incorrect signal levels**: Adjust gain settings on the MAM or FCU as needed.
*   **Noise or interference**: Consult the provided noise reduction manual for guidance.
