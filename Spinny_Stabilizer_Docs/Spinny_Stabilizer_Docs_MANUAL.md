## Getting Started

Follow these steps to get up and running in minutes.

### Step 1: Charge the Device

Before first use, fully charge your Spinny Stabilizer using the included USB-C cable. The Status LED will blink while charging and turn solid green when fully charged. A full charge takes approximately 2 hours.

### Step 2: Unfold and Mount Your Phone

The Spinny Stabilizer is designed to be compact. Unfold the main arm until it clicks into place. Open the phone clamp and place your smartphone inside. Ensure the phone's camera is on the left side, facing away from you.

### Step 3: Balance Your Phone

**This is the most important step for smooth operation!**

With the stabilizer powered **OFF**, adjust your phone's position.

*   Slide the phone left or right within the clamp.
*   The goal is to have the phone remain perfectly level and balanced on its own without tilting to either side.
*   An unbalanced phone will strain the motors, reduce battery life, and result in shaky footage.

### Step 4: Power On

Once your phone is balanced, press and hold the **Power Button** for 3 seconds. The gimbal will spring to life and stabilize your phone. You're ready to shoot!

## Basic Operation

### Stabilizer Modes

The Spinny Stabilizer has three primary modes. Single-press the **Mode Button** to cycle through them.

*   **Pan Follow (PF) Mode (Default):** The gimbal follows your panning (left/right) movements smoothly while keeping the tilt and roll locked. The Status LED will be solid green.
*   **Lock (L) Mode:** All three axes are locked. The camera will remain pointed in one direction, no matter how you move the handle. The Status LED will be solid blue.
*   **Full Follow (FPV) Mode:** All three axes (pan, tilt, and roll) will follow your movements, allowing for dynamic, first-person-view shots. The Status LED will be solid red.

### Using the Joystick and Buttons

| Action                        | Button/Control      | Result                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pan/Tilt Manually**         | Joystick            | Move the joystick to manually aim the camera.                       |
| **Cycle Modes**               | Mode Button (1x)    | Switch between PF, L, and FPV modes.                                |
| **Switch Orientation**        | Mode Button (2x)    | Instantly switch between horizontal and vertical (portrait) shooting. |
| **Re-center Gimbal**          | Trigger Button (2x) | Returns the gimbal to its default forward-facing position.          |
| **Enter/Exit Lock Mode**      | Trigger (Press/Hold)| Temporarily locks all axes. Release to return to the previous mode. |
| **Start/Stop Recording**      | Record Button (1x)  | Starts or stops video recording (requires app connection).          |
| **Take Photo**                | Record Button (1x)  | Takes a photo when in photo mode (requires app connection).         |
| **Power On/Off**              | Power Button (Hold) | Turns the device on or off.                                         |

## Advanced Techniques

### Inception Mode (360° Roll)

1.  Enter **FPV Mode**.
2.  Hold the stabilizer horizontally (like a flashlight).
3.  Push the joystick fully to the left or right. The gimbal will perform a continuous 360-degree barrel roll for a mind-bending effect.

### Underslung Mode (Low Angle)

For dramatic low-angle shots, simply flip the gimbal upside down. The stabilizer will automatically detect the orientation and keep the camera upright. This is perfect for tracking pets, children, or for creative ground-level shots.

## Maintenance & Firmware

*   **Cleaning:** Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth to clean the device. Do not use liquid cleaners.
*   **Storage:** When not in use, store the stabilizer in its folded position inside the provided pouch to protect the motors.
*   **Firmware Updates:** We periodically release firmware updates to improve performance and add features. The SpinnyGo app will notify you when an update is available. Follow the on-screen instructions to update. **Do not turn off the stabilizer or your phone during an update.**
