### 1.0 Introduction

The Cellular Regenesis Chamber Model CRC-M7 is a mechanical medical device designed for the accelerated healing of severe biological trauma. It is not intended for curing genetic diseases, chronic illnesses, or reversing the natural aging process.

The chamber functions by immersing the subject in a nanite-infused, oxygenated amniotic suspension (Regenesis Gel). A high-fidelity bio-scanner maps the subject's damaged tissues against their own healthy cellular patterns. Micro-manipulator arms and targeted sonic resonance frequencies then direct the activated nanites to debride necrotic tissue, repair cellular structures, and stimulate rapid, scar-free healing.

### 4.0 Critical Safety Precautions

*   **WARNING:** Never operate the chamber with a subject containing any metallic implants, shrapnel, or foreign objects unless cleared by a full-system diagnostic. The bio-scanner's magnetic field can cause severe internal injury.
*   **WARNING:** Ensure the subject is completely unconscious or sedated for the entire duration of the cycle. Involuntary movement can disrupt the nanite calibration and cause tissue damage.
*   **CAUTION:** Always verify fluid levels before initiating a cycle. Low gel or catalyst levels will result in an incomplete or failed procedure.
*   **CAUTION:** Do not attempt to override safety interlocks. They are in place to prevent pod pressurization failure, atmospheric contamination, and cycle initiation with an unsecured hatch.
*   **NOTICE:** The CRC-M7 requires a stable, level foundation capable of supporting its wet weight.

### 5.0 Pre-Operation Checklist

1.  [ ] **Physical Inspection:** Walk around the unit. Check for leaks, damaged cables, or visible stress on the chassis or viewport.
2.  [ ] **Power On:** Activate the main breaker, then power on the Control Console. Wait for the system to pass its initial self-test (approx. 90 seconds).
3.  [ ] **Verify Consumables:** Check the levels of RG-4 Gel, NCS-B2 Catalyst, and Sterilization Fluid on the console's main screen. Refill if any are below 25%.
4.  [ ] **Calibrate Scanners:** Run the "Scanner Calibration" diagnostic from the maintenance menu. This should be done once every 24 hours or before each new subject.
5.  [ ] **Prepare Subject:** Ensure the subject is stripped of all clothing, jewelry, and metallic objects. Connect them to the external vital signs monitor.

### 6.0 Standard Operating Procedure

1.  **Login:** Authenticate on the Control Console using your certified technician credentials.
2.  **Open Hatch:** Select "Open Hatch" on the console. Stand clear as the hydraulic door retracts.
3.  **Position Subject:** Carefully transfer the subject onto the pod's integrated support cradle. Secure the subject using the non-metallic restraints.
4.  **Close & Seal Hatch:** Ensure all personnel are clear of the hatch path. Select "Close & Seal Hatch" on the console. Visually confirm that all three (3) locking indicators on the console turn green.
5.  **Initiate Diagnostic Scan:** Select "Begin Diagnostic Scan." The chamber will perform a 5-minute high-resolution scan to identify all areas of trauma.
6.  **Review Regenesis Plan:** The console will display a 3D model of the subject with highlighted trauma zones and a proposed treatment plan, including estimated time and consumable usage. **You must review and approve this plan.** You can manually exclude non-critical areas to prioritize treatment if necessary.
7.  **Engage Primary Cycle:** Once the plan is approved, press the "ENGAGE CYCLE" button. The chamber will begin filling with Regenesis Gel and injecting the Nanite Catalyst.
8.  **Monitor:** Remain present and monitor the subject's vitals, nanite activity, and tissue integrity percentage on the main console display. Do not leave the chamber unattended.
9.  **Cycle Completion:** The system will sound a chime and display "CYCLE COMPLETE." The gel will automatically drain back into the reservoir for filtration.

### 7.0 Post-Operation Procedures

1.  **Wait for Venting:** The system will vent the pod atmosphere to remove residual ozone and aerosols. Wait for the "ALL CLEAR" indicator on the console (approx. 3 minutes).
2.  **Open Hatch:** Select "Open Hatch."
3.  **Remove Subject:** Carefully unstrap and remove the subject from the support cradle. Transfer to a recovery gurney. The subject will be coated in a thin layer of inert gel, which can be wiped away.
4.  **Initiate Sterilization Cycle:** Close the hatch and select "Maintenance" -> "Run Sterilization Cycle." This is a mandatory 45-minute process that uses UV-C light and sterilization fluid to clean the pod interior and filters.
